Our Story

Real people. Real stories. Real places.

Instead of ten thousand reviews, one friend.

Most of us choose where to go by scrolling: ranked lists, star ratings and the same few viral cafés.

The Travel Letter Club works the other way round. You are matched with someone who lives abroad, and you come to know their place through their eyes: the bakery they pass every morning, the tradition their family keeps, the bench where they go when they need a little quiet.

Letter by letter, a stranger becomes a familiar face, and a dot on the map becomes somewhere that matters to you. When you feel ready, you can visit with a local guide written by the person who calls it home.

ConnectionOne person, getting to know each other over time.
CuriosityQuestions about daily life, not just landmarks.
ExchangeBoth sides share their culture and learn.
DiscoveryPlaces chosen by someone who loves them.

How It Works

Four steps from stranger to friend.

No itineraries and no algorithms. Just a person, a pen and a place worth knowing.

  1. 1

    Get matched with someone abroad

    Tell us what you love and where you are curious about. We pair you with a pen pal from another country.

  2. 2

    Exchange letters and personal stories

    Write about everyday life, local traditions and favourite cafés. Slow, thoughtful and personal.

  3. 3

    Receive a monthly cultural box

    Each month a box arrives with a letter, a local treat, a photograph and a small keepsake.

  4. 4

    Explore their city, if you wish

    Your pen pal writes a personal local guide. Follow it from home, or use it to visit.

A Letter From Somewhere Else

Open it slowly. It was written for you.

This is a fictional example of the kind of letter a pen pal in Copenhagen might send.

From · Copenhagen, DenmarkTo · a friend abroad

There's a tiny bakery near the canal that smells like cinnamon every morning. On Sundays, my friends and I walk by the water and talk about everything.

If you ever visit, I'll show you my favourite spot to watch the sunset. It's not in any guidebook — it's just where I go when I need a little quiet.

See you somewhere, soon ♡

Discover a city through a local.

The places a pen pal might share are the ones that never make a top-ten list.

1The neighbourhood café

The one with mismatched chairs

The owner knows everyone's order, and the best seat is the one by the window. Ask what is fresh from the oven.

“Come before nine.”
2A family tradition

Friday dinner at Grandma's

Everyone brings one dish. Nobody leaves hungry, and the recipes are never written down.

3A favourite sunset spot

A bench with no sign

A quiet stretch of water with no crowds. Best twenty minutes before the sun goes down.

4A hidden bookshop or market

Up the narrow staircase

Secondhand cookbooks, a handwritten price list and a shop cat that decides who stays.

5After class

The steps by the old library

Bikes everywhere, someone always has snacks, and the plan for the evening gets made here.
